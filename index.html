Manufacturing Process

Our manufacturing process includes the following stages:

    Blow Room

  • Toyoda machinery setup. Mainly purpose of this department to cleane and open the raw material into small tufts

    • Carding

  • Suarer card + Trutzschler + Crosrol + Toyoda. Carding machines clean and fiber to fiber open the raw material and transfer into sliver form

    • Drawing

    Rieter + DX 8 + China TMF D81 L + Toyoda + Howa. Drawing machines are used to mix the different type of sliver come from carding department.

    Roving

  • Toyoda FL16 + Howa RME. Light twisting and drafting Performed

    • Ring Spinning

  • Toyoda RY + RY4 + RY5. Ring spinning proseses a major roll in yarn manufacturing. In this department machines exerts draft and twist to finalize the yarn formation.

    • Winding

  • Muretic Q pro + Savio Polar + Machconer 7-II. in this department yarn is being winding on Paper cone from ring bobbin. During windings yarn faults are removed. Desire weight and length attained.

    • Packing

  • finally the Cones taken from Autocone department are packed in bags according to required no of packings like 24 cone packing or 40 cone packing. Normally one bag is 100 lb.
